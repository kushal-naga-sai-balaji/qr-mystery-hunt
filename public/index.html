📜 Field Operative Briefing

Welcome to Project Chronos. You are tasked with cracking 5 sequential security checkpoints distributed across the operational area.

Rule 1: The Three Portals

Each QR checkpoint encodes three distinct portal links (Portal A, Portal B, Portal C). When scanned with any smartphone camera or QR reader, all three links will be presented.

Rule 2: Authentic vs Decoy

Every link hosts an interactive puzzle. However, two links are deceptive decoys! Solving a decoy triggers a decoy alarm and wastes valuable time. Only the single authentic portal yields the Stage Passkey and location clue to the next QR checkpoint.

Rule 3: Progression

Solve Stage 1 to locate QR 2. Solve Stage 2 to locate QR 3. Complete all 5 stages to unlock the Master Vault in Stage 5!
